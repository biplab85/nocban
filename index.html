Our HistoryEst. 1979

A Legacy of Excellence

The Bangladesh Olympic Association was established in 1979, following the country's recognition by the International Olympic Committee. Since its inception, BOA has been dedicated to promoting Olympic values and developing sports across the nation.

Over the past four decades, we have nurtured countless athletes who have represented Bangladesh with pride on regional and international stages. Our commitment to excellence, sportsmanship, and national development through athletics remains unwavering.
